About STEM Academy

Nurturing Brain Power & Memory Excellence

STEM Abacus (Smart Enliven Memory) in Woraiyur is dedicated to building strong mathematical foundation and language skills in kids. We provide specialized training systems that sharpen cognitive faculties like auditory memory, concentration, processing speed, and mental visualization.

Through our scientifically structured syllabus, we transform learning from a stressful task into a fun, rewarding hobby. Whether it is mastering rapid mental calculations or refining handwriting legibility, our batches offer a supportive learning environment.

Academic Programs

Core Development Courses

Specially curated courses targeting critical foundational skills in growing children.

Mental Abacus Arithmetic

Ages: 5 to 14 Years

A cognitive development program using the Japanese soroban abacus. It trains children to visualize the abacus mentally, allowing them to solve complex math operations (addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division) at speed without paper or calculators.

8 Level Program Sharpen focus & memory Certificate on level completion
Syllabus & Details

Levels 1-2 cover basic single-digit additions & subtractions. Levels 3-5 introduce double-digit operations, multiplications, and divisions. Levels 6-8 focus on advanced decimals, rapid mental calculation techniques, and visualization speed runs.

Timings: Saturday 4-6 PM or Sunday 10-12 AM Fees: Discuss on inquiry

Speed & Vedic Maths

Ages: 10+ Years

Designed for older students to eliminate math fear and prepare for competitive benchmarks. Covers rapid calculation tricks, finger counting techniques, Vedic sutras, and fraction hacks that boost arithmetic speed by up to 1500%.

Mental shortcuts Ideal for school exams Boosts logical thinking
Syllabus & Details

Covers Vedic Multiplication methods (Ekadhikena, Nikhalarn), rapid squaring/cubing, fast division techniques, percentage hacks, and algebraic shortcuts. Includes timed speed trials to monitor speed enhancements.

Timings: Saturday 2-4 PM or Sunday 2-4 PM Fees: Discuss on inquiry

English Phonics Reading

Ages: 4 to 8 Years

Builds strong pronunciation, spelling, and reading skills. Students learn the 44 basic phonics sounds, blending rules, digraphs, and tricky words, helping them read storybooks independently and spell words accurately without memorizing letters.

Better pronunciation Vocabulary enrichment Independent reading habits
Syllabus & Details

Covers basic letter sounds (A-Z), short and long vowel blending, digraphs (sh, ch, th), vowel teams (ai, ee, oa), and tricky sight words. Focuses on structured phonics flashcards and reading storybooks.

Timings: Weekdays (Mon & Wed) 5-6 PM Fees: Discuss on inquiry

Handwriting Improvement

Ages: 6+ Years

Assists children in developing neat, legible, and attractive print and cursive styles. Focuses on pen/pencil grip correction, wrist posture, letter sizing, slant consistency, and writing speed adjustments.

Cursive & Print training Pencil grip correction Boost exam presentation
Syllabus & Details

Focuses on strokes, loops, and connections for cursive writing, letter height consistency, word spacing, and pen grip alignment. Includes daily drills, speed writing practices, and exam sheet presentation styles.

Timings: Weekdays (Tue & Thu) 5-6 PM or Sat 10-12 AM Fees: Discuss on inquiry
